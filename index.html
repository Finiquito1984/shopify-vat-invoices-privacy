Privacy Policy — EU VAT Invoices for Shopify

Last updated 25 November 2026.

EU VAT Invoices for Shopify ("the extension") is an independent browser extension published by Artur Santos. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Shopify, Intuit (QuickBooks) or Xero.

What the extension does

When you click the extension icon on your Shopify admin Orders page and press Read orders, the extension reads the orders you selected from the Shopify admin tab you have open, using the session you are already signed in with. It then builds invoice and credit-note PDFs and accounting exports on your computer. You can also import a Shopify Orders export CSV instead of reading the admin directly.

Data we collect

None of your Shopify order data is collected by us. We do not operate a server for invoice generation. Your orders, invoices, customer details, VAT numbers, seller settings and Shopify credentials are never sent to us.

  • The extension never sees or stores your Shopify password.
  • All processing (reading orders, applying VAT treatments, building PDFs, CSVs and the ZIP) happens locally in your browser.
  • Network requests for order reading go only to the Shopify admin you are already signed in to, and only when you start a read.
  • The optional VIES check (below) sends only a customer's country code and VAT number to the European Commission, and only when you click it.

Data stored on your device

The extension stores, in your browser's local extension storage only: your seller settings (business details, logo, numbering and accounting mappings); invoice and credit-note numbering state; a monthly counter for the free-plan limit; your plan status from the payment provider once Pro is active; and the last two order batches so you can re-generate; your last chosen period and output options (file-name pattern, export formats); and VIES check results for 24 hours. Older batches are deleted automatically. You can delete this data by removing the extension.

VIES VAT number check (optional)

In the invoice preview you can click Check (or Check all B2B VAT numbers in VIES) to verify a business customer's EU VAT number. The extension then sends only the country code and the VAT number (for example FR + 40303265045) directly from your browser to the European Commission's official VIES service at https://ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs/vies/. No customer name, address, order or invoice data is sent, and nothing passes through us. The European Commission processes the request under its own privacy statement for VIES. The answer (valid / not valid / unavailable, and the registered name if VIES returns it) is kept in your browser's local extension storage for 24 hours so the same number is not checked twice. Chrome asks you once for permission to contact this service; if you decline, no check is made.

Payments

If you buy Pro, payment is handled by ExtensionPay, under its own privacy policy. ExtensionPay (and its payment processor, Stripe) may receive your email address and payment details to process the purchase and verify your licence. We never receive your card details. The extension only receives a yes/no paid status.

Pro costs 7.99 USD per month or 59 USD per year. There is no quarterly plan. The free tier remains 25 new documents per month; re-downloading already numbered documents stays free.

Contact form

The form below (and the same form in the extension Settings) sends your subject, message, and optional reply address through a form-forwarding service (Web3Forms, under that service's privacy policy) so the publisher can read your message. This page does not publish a contact email address. If you include a reply address, it is used only to answer you.
